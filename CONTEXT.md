# Personal Archive

Personal Archive is a public collection of dated long-form writing. Its language distinguishes content state from the interface that presents it.

## Language

**博文（Post）**:
A dated, titled piece of long-form writing with a category, summary, and body.
_Avoid_: Article, Entry, 日志条目

**档案（Archive）**:
The public collection of published posts, ordered from newest to oldest.
_Avoid_: Feed, Journal, 文章流

**草稿（Draft）**:
A post that is intentionally excluded from the public archive.
_Avoid_: Hidden post, Unpublished article

**发布（Publish）**:
The transition that makes a post available in the public archive.
_Avoid_: Upload, Sync, 自动读取
