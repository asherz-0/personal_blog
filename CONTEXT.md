# Personal Archive

Personal Archive is a public collection of dated long-form writing. Its language distinguishes content state from the interface that presents it.

## Language

**博文（Post）**:
A dated, titled piece of long-form writing with a category, tags, summary, and body.
_Avoid_: Article, Entry, 日志条目

**输入条目（Consumption）**:
A dated book, essay, video, podcast, or reference selected because it changed a question, judgment, or action. It has a source, summary, and tags, and may link to its original location.
_Avoid_: Post, Bookmark, Reading log, 收藏

**标签（Tag）**:
A reusable label attached to a Post or Consumption. Tags group related content within its collection and do not replace a Post's broader category.
_Avoid_: Category, Folder, 目录

**档案（Archive）**:
The public collection of published posts, ordered from newest to oldest.
_Avoid_: Feed, Journal, 文章流

**草稿（Draft）**:
A post that is intentionally excluded from the public archive.
_Avoid_: Hidden post, Unpublished article

**发布（Publish）**:
The transition that makes a post available in the public archive.
_Avoid_: Upload, Sync, 自动读取
